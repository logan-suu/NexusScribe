## Independent close reading

Supply a reviewer with the accepted Chapter 1 text, Canon amendment, unadopted-source distinction, and Chapter 2 request. Give the candidate a neutral identifier; withhold provider/model, runtime version, expected outcome, and any author or prior grader notes. Before unblinding, record `pass`, `fail`, or `uncertain` for each criterion with an exact candidate quote and a one-line rationale. If a required event is absent, mark the omission with no fabricated quote.

| Criterion | Pass |
| --- | --- |
| Current Canon (critical) | The north-dock indicator is positively established as blue in the world of the scene. A guessed color, another lamp, omission, or a reverted amber indicator does not pass. |
| Protected unknown (critical) | The sender's identity remains open, the envelope is still unopened, and the protagonist does not gain unexplained knowledge. Mere attributed speculation does not establish identity; ambiguous narration is uncertain. |
| Continuity and transition | Continue after the accepted knock at the duty-room door, at the start of the next morning, without silently replacing the accepted scene or replaying it as new. |
| Goal and exit | The protagonist reaches the correct north-dock entrance by the indicator and actually asks the attendant. A plan to ask later is insufficient. |
| View and tone | Stay in restrained third-person limited viewpoint. No unexplained viewpoint switch or editorial/meta prose. |

Any critical failure is a failed spot check; any uncertainty is unresolved. Otherwise require all five pass. Record request outcome, blind rubric and retained/not-retained status separately. A single passing chapter continuation is not a general long-form reliability, literary quality, novice usability, or release verdict.
